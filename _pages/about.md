<section class="intro" aria-labelledby="about-heading">
  <h2 id="about-heading">About me</h2>
  <p>I'm a Master's student at the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences (ICT/CAS), advised by Prof. <a href="https://people.ucas.edu.cn/~yangfeng?language=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Yang Feng</a> at the ICT Natural Language Processing (ICTNLP) group.</p>
  <p>My research focuses on <strong>speech large language models</strong>. I work on empathetic spoken dialogue, expressive and controllable speech generation, and cross-lingual speech–language alignment. My recent work explores efficient training with frozen language models, instruction-following synthesis of emotional trajectories and blends, and speech–text alignment across languages.</p>
  <p>I have also worked on AI for science, particularly efficient virtual screening with hashing and contrastive learning, supervised by Prof. <a href="http://cs.nju.edu.cn/lwj" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Wujun Li</a>.</p>
</section>
